Cluedle

Wordle for armchair detectives: a daily 1940s-noir mystery, worked on a murder board, where you stamp who did it and why in five guesses or fewer.

The core journeyFrom case file to countdown

Today's case arrives as a closed case file: case number, date, title, and Open the file.

First visit only: a short how-to-play covering the stamps, the five guesses, the hidden cipher, and the red string. Reopenable from the board.

The murder board: newspaper-clipping story, 3–4 suspect cards, motive cards, witness statements, the stamp tray, the red-string spool, and five guesses. Draw string between pins to think.

Spot the hidden scrawl, click it, and the decoder card comes forward. Set the wheel to the key from the story and press Decode. Three tries; each miss blots out more for good. Never costs a guess.

Stamp GUILTY on a suspect and a motive, then confirm. Each stamp changes color to show which half was right. A miss pins up a new plain clue.

Stamp LIAR on a witness statement and pick what really happened. Free, one shot. The stamp's color shows whether you were right.

CASE CLOSED with what happened, who you saved, and your rank, or GONE COLD with who did it, why, and what it cost. Then Copy share card.

Reopen the same day: your finished board, the ending, and a countdown to the next case.

Behavior and how we'll know it worksFeatures

Accusing with the GUILTY stamp scope › Core Loop
  • Stamp a suspect card and a motive card, then confirm. Uses one of five guesses.
  • Each stamp changes color: suspect right or wrong, motive right or wrong.
  • Past stamps stay on the board as a record.
  • One confirm uses exactly one guess.
  • Suspect and motive stamps show right or wrong independently.
  • Earlier stamps stay visible after later guesses.
Clues unlocking scope › Core Loop
  • Each wrong accusation pins up the case's next plain clue.
  • After each wrong guess, one new clue appears.
Catching the liar with the LIAR stamp scope › Unique Kernel
  • Stamp LIAR on a statement, then pick what really happened from a few options.
  • Free, one shot per case. The stamp's color shows right or wrong.
  • Guesses remaining don't change.
  • The LIAR stamp can't be used a second time.
  • A correct catch counts toward the rank.
Finding and cracking the cipher scope › Unique Kernel
  • Hidden on the board from the start as part of the scenery; nothing announces it.
  • The wheel never unscrambles live; Decode commits.
  • Three tries. Each wrong try permanently blots out more of the message. Three misses: it stays scrambled.
  • Never costs a guess; the case is solvable without it.
  • Turning the wheel alone never reveals readable text.
  • First-try crack shows the full message.
  • A later crack shows more blotted out.
  • After three misses, Decode is gone.
  • Cipher tries never change guesses.
Red string scope › POC Boundary
  • Draw string between any two pins; remove it. Only for the player's thinking.
  • String stays until removed.
  • String never affects guesses, rank, or outcome.
Case ending scope › What "Working" Looks Like
  • CASE CLOSED: what really happened, who was saved, the rank.
  • GONE COLD: the culprit, motive, what happened, and the cost. "It was Martha… not catching her cost 7 innocent people their lives."
  • The fifth miss shows the gone-cold ending with the true answer.
  • A right guess shows the closed ending with the rank.
Daily case, case file, how to play scope › POC Boundary
  • One case per day, the same for everyone, chosen by date from three cases.
  • Case file first; how-to-play on the first visit only, reopenable from the board.
  • Same date, same case; different dates, different cases.
  • First visit: file → how to play → board. Later visits skip the how-to-play.
States what if?
  • Mid-case return: closing and reopening the same day keeps everything, so reloading never resets tries.
  • Finished, same day: finished board, ending, countdown.
  • New day: the next case's file.
  • Copy fails: the card text is shown to copy by hand.

Ladder

Five ranks

  1. Amateur Sleuth 0–1
  2. Private Eye 2–3
  3. Hard-Boiled Investigator 4–5
  4. Seasoned Detective 6–7
  5. Basically Sherlock 8–10
Points

Hidden points, 0–10

  • Guesses left after solving: 0–4
  • Cipher: 3 first try, 2 second, 1 third, 0 not cracked
  • Liar caught: 3

Only a solved case earns a rank. Values and bands are a starting point, tuned while playtesting in 5-build.

The boundaryBuilding, deferred, not doing

Building The proof of concept

Every feature above, three hand-written cases (Claude drafts, you edit), as a static web app in the browser. No AI at runtime.

Deferred Not now, with reasons
  • Red-string physics: polish after the core works.
  • Correct string links counting toward rank: needs "correct" links defined.
  • Cases four through seven: content after three polished ones.
  • Streaks and stats across days: not needed to prove the kernel.
  • Later ideas: a case archive; typewriter, stamp, and rain sounds.
Not doing Non-goals
  • Accusing by dragging red string: string is for thinking.
  • Accounts or a server: progress stays on the device.
  • Live unscrambling as the wheel turns: makes brute force trivial.
  • AI at runtime.

Before 4-specOpen questions

None blocking. Can wait for 5-build:

  • How hidden the cipher scrawl should be, tuned while writing and playtesting the cases.
  • Rank point values and bands, tuned while playtesting.
